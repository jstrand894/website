Master’s Thesis Defense

The Role of Chemical Ecology and Agroecological Importance of Smooth Brome in Biological Control of the Wheat Stem Sawfly by Native Parasitoids

I successfully defended my Master’s thesis at Montana State University on August 19, 2024. My research focused on the agroecological role of smooth brome (Bromus inermis) in supporting native parasitoids of the wheat stem sawfly (Cephus cinctus), with a particular emphasis on agro- and chemical ecology.
